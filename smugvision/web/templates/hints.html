Hints

A hint is something you know about a photo that the model does not. It is treated as ground truth and outranks the model's own visual guess — which is how you stop a Nylabone being captioned as "a long cracker". Hints are saved to ~/.smugvision/hints.yaml and are used by the command line tool as well as this web UI.

Scopes are applied most general first: global then album then image. All of the ones that apply to a frame are combined, so a global note and an image note are both sent to the model.

Global note

global scope

Applies to every image in every album, including every album you process in future. Keep it to durable facts — who your family are, what your pets look like — not anything specific to one shoot.

Leave empty and save to remove the global note.

Saved album and image notes

Album notes apply to one album; image notes apply to a single frame. The quickest way to add an image note is from a proof sheet, where you can see the photograph while you write it.

Pets

Face recognition learns human faces from reference photos, so an animal is invisible to it however often it appears. Name your pets here and they become tickable on every frame of a proof sheet, next to the people.
